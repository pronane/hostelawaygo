A hostel-native event guide with two live windows: the city you're in tonight, and the city you're arriving in on Tuesday.

  • Breadth — hostel events and the city's own festivals, gigs and markets in one timeline.
  • Both windows — no paying for a pass, no losing the last city when you switch.
  • Traveller lens — is it free, can I walk in alone, how far from my bed, will I make the last metro.
  • Supply side — hostels post tonight's thing in 60 seconds. Every bed is a channel.
All names, venues and numbers are mock data. Tap a card for the detail sheet, tap a tab, filter with the chips. Nothing is connected to a backend.
